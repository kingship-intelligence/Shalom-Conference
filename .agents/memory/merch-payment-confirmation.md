---
name: Merch payment confirmation
description: The payment-confirmation rule for Shalom's manual Cash App preorder flow.
---

Cash App preorder submission confirms that the order was received, not that payment is complete. Finance must verify the payment manually before an order can be described as paid.

**Why:** The conference intentionally uses a simple Cash App handoff rather than an integrated payment processor, so the site has no reliable automated payment status.

**How to apply:** Customer-facing copy and receipt emails must distinguish “preorder received” from “payment verified.” Keep finance in the review loop before giving a paid/fulfilled confirmation.
